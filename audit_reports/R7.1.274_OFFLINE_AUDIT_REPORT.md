## Root-cause fixes
- Trade PnL is no longer fabricated from account-wide balance deltas. Incomplete trade-local fills are stored as NULL / UNRECONCILED and excluded from win/loss/PnL analytics.
- Market-order acknowledgements no longer treat requested price or requested amount as actual fills. Residual PnL requires a confirmed exit quantity and price.
- Bybit closed-PnL matching excludes imported EXCHANGE mirror rows, targets bot-owned trades, and updates capital-pool accounting only after authoritative reconciliation.
- If exchange PnL supersedes a previously applied estimate, the capital pool applies only the PnL difference for that trade ID, idempotently.
- HYBRID preferred margin scales as configured preference × per-position slot capital / 15 USDT, as a diagnostic only; risk-derived target margin and risk/slot limits remain authoritative. FIXED_MARGIN stays literal.
- Runtime/history dashboards exclude legacy balance-delta estimates and unresolved partial PnL from numeric PnL analytics.
- R7.1.273 AUTO leverage cap and RSI=50 neutral logic are preserved.
