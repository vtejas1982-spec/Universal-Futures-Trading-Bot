## Protection / risk audit

- Normal protection remains single-owner: HOLD-SL > ATR SL > ROI SL > fallback.
- ATR TP remains derived from the resolved SL distance; TP2 must be beyond TP1.
- Fixed Qty remains literal requested quantity and is checked against the configured risk ceiling and leverage-tier cap before entry and again after fill.
- Equity-risk sizing uses the resolved stop distance.
- Cost gate and liquidation gate remain downstream hard gates.
- TP1/TP2 quantities must match the configured split and actual filled position quantity.
- TP1 break-even replaces the old SL only after the replacement SL is verified active.
- AI trailing only tightens the active stop and is checked against liquidation safety.
- Hold-All-Reverse disables normal TP management as an explicit separate protection contract.
