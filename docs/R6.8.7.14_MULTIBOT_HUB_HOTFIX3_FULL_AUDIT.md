## HOTFIX3

### Fixed — liquidation data quality

A Demo log exposed an exchange-reported XAUT liquidation price around `191802.875111` against an entry around `4164.6`, which produced an implausible liquidation distance. HOTFIX3 classifies an exchange `liqPrice` as unusable when it is missing/non-finite, geometrically on the wrong side of entry, or farther than the 75% sanity limit.

When exchange liquidation data is unusable, the bot uses the deterministic leverage/buffer safety envelope. This does not loosen liquidation protection and does not change selected leverage.

### Exact leverage

`AI_AGENT_AUTO_LOWER_LEVERAGE_ON_SL_BLOCK = False`

The GUI-selected leverage is authoritative. Unsafe AI/Fibonacci protection at that leverage fails closed.
